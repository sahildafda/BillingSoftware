1. Getting started

MotoDhandho helps you keep your shop data in one place and complete sales quickly.

Create your account

  1. Open MotoDhandho and select Create an account.
  2. Enter your company name, GST number, owner name, email, and shop address.
  3. Choose a password and confirm it.
  4. Check your email for the verification code and enter the code in MotoDhandho.
  5. Return to the sign-in screen and log in with your email and password.
Good to knowKeep your login email and password private. A session normally stays active for seven days on the same computer.

Navigation

After signing in, use the left menu to open Dashboard, Billing, Products, Customers, Suppliers, or Reports. Select your account picture for backup, theme, and logout options.

2. Dashboard

The Dashboard gives you a quick view of your business. Choose This month, This year, or All time, then choose the view you need.

Business summary

See revenue, number of orders, customers, total stock, low-stock products, and out-of-stock products.

Sales view

See orders and revenue over time. Move your pointer over a chart point for exact details.

Payments view

See how much was collected by cash, online payment, and card.

Inventory view

See product count, low stock, out-of-stock items, and top-selling products.

Select Refresh whenever you want the latest numbers.

Sales Assistant

Open the round assistant button to view recent sales insights. You can ask simple questions such as:

  • What products are in demand?
  • Which products have low demand?
  • What should I reorder?
  • Who are my top customers?
NoteThe Sales Assistant uses your billing data and provides focused business insights. It is not a general-purpose chat service.

3. Set up your business data

For the smoothest billing experience, add suppliers first, then products and customers.

Suppliers

  1. Open Suppliers and select Add supplier.
  2. Enter supplier name, company name, contact number, and email. Address and notes are optional.
  3. Select Save. Use search, sorting, edit, or delete when needed.

Products

  1. Open Products and select Add product.
  2. Enter product name and select a supplier. A supplier is required.
  3. Enter cost price, selling price, stock quantity, GST percentage, and discount percentage.
  4. Optionally add product images and internal reference details.
  5. Select Save. MotoDhandho creates a barcode automatically for the product.

You can search products, filter by brand, sort by name or price, edit details, delete products, and print barcode labels in small, medium, or large sizes with the number of copies you need.

ImportantDeleting a product is permanent. Check the product before confirming deletion.

Customers

  1. Open Customers and select Add customer.
  2. Enter customer name, contact number, email, and starting credit if applicable.
  3. Optionally add firm name and GST number, then select Save.

Customer records can be searched, sorted, edited, and deleted. A saved customer is useful for credit, receipts, and returns.

4. Create a bill

Billing is designed for fast checkout. You can bill a walk-in customer or choose a saved customer.

  1. Open Billing.
  2. Choose a saved customer, or leave the customer as a walk-in customer. You can also create a new customer from the billing screen.
  3. Add products by searching their name, or scan/type the barcode and press Enter. A barcode scanner can paste the code directly.
  4. Review the cart. Change quantities or remove an item when needed.
  5. Open payment and select Cash, Online, or Card. You may add more than one payment row for split payments.
  6. Apply available customer credit if the customer has credit.
  7. Enter the amount received. MotoDhandho shows balance due, change, and the final total.
  8. Complete the sale only when the payment covers the amount due. MotoDhandho saves the invoice and reduces stock automatically.

Payment example

A customer owes Rs. 1,000. Enter Rs. 700 as cash and Rs. 300 as card. The bill is fully paid.

Change example

A customer owes Rs. 450 and gives Rs. 500 cash. MotoDhandho shows Rs. 50 as change.

Receipt

After saving an invoice, open it from Reports to view the receipt and select Print. Use the browser print dialog to print or save the receipt.

GST and pricesProduct prices are treated as GST-inclusive. MotoDhandho does not add GST again during billing. The GST fields help classify products and reports, but the current invoice calculation does not add a separate GST amount.
Common messagesIf an item has insufficient stock, a barcode is not found, the payment is incomplete, or your session has expired, MotoDhandho shows a message on the billing screen. Fix the message and try again.

5. Reports, receipts, and returns

Open Reports to find invoices and understand your sales.

Find an invoice

Filter by invoice status, current or last month, current or last calendar year, current or last financial year, or a custom date range. You can also filter by GST or non-GST classification and search by invoice number, customer, GST number, status, or payment method.

View and print

  1. Find the invoice using filters or search.
  2. Select View to open the receipt.
  3. Select Print and choose your printer, or choose the browser option to save as PDF.

Export report data

Use the report export controls to download a CSV file or open a print-ready report. The CSV file can be opened in Excel or another spreadsheet application.

Export wordingThe Excel option currently creates a CSV file. The PDF option opens a formatted print window; it does not directly download a PDF file. Choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog.

Return items

  1. Open a saved-customer invoice in Reports.
  2. Select Return and enter the quantity for each item being returned.
  3. Select Process return.

The returned quantity goes back into stock, and the returned amount is added to that saved customer's credit. Walk-in invoices cannot be returned through the screen.

6. Backup and restore

Keep a regular copy of your business database, especially before a major change or computer maintenance.

Download a backup

  1. Select your account picture in the bottom-left area.
  2. Select Export Database Backup or Download Backup.
  3. Store the downloaded database file in a secure location.

Import a backup

  1. Open the account menu and select Import Database.
  2. Choose a compatible .sqlite, .db, or .sqlite3 file.
  3. Wait for the confirmation, then allow MotoDhandho to reload.
ImportantImport replaces the current database with the selected data. Use a backup first and only import a file you trust. An invalid or incompatible file will be rejected.

Theme

Use the account menu to switch between light mode and dark mode. The choice changes the appearance of the application and does not change your business data.

7. Updates and logout

Check for updates

The packaged Windows application checks for updates when it starts and every four hours. To check manually, use Help > Check for updates or press Ctrl+Shift+U. You can choose when to download and restart.

Before restartingFinish or note any unfinished billing work before installing an update. MotoDhandho creates and checks a database snapshot before an update is installed.

Log out

Select your account picture and choose Logout. MotoDhandho attempts a backup before clearing the local login session. You will return to the sign-in screen.

8. Helpful notes

Situation What to do
Barcode does not find a product Check the code, confirm the product exists, or search by product name. The product must have been saved first.
Payment cannot be completed Make sure the payment total plus eligible customer credit covers the final amount.
Customer cannot be saved Check that name, phone, and email are filled in and that the record is not a duplicate.
Product cannot be saved Choose a supplier and check that prices, stock, GST, and discount are not negative.
Report looks empty Change the date range or remove a filter, then refresh the report.
Session has expired Log in again. Save or note any unfinished work before leaving the screen.

Current scope

MotoDhandho currently focuses on retail billing and inventory. It does not include supplier purchase orders, stock receiving, payable management, automatic refunds, or an in-app password-change page.
